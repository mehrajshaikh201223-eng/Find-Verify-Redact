Detection order Exact Precedence Evaluation

Higher-priority detectors claim matching byte spans first. Overlapping lower-priority pattern candidates are safely dropped to eliminate collision artifacts.

01 / CC Mod-10 Checksum

Credit Card (Luhn Verified)

Captures 13–19 digit spans with optional single spaces or hyphens using negative lookarounds. Verifies mathematical mod-10 Luhn validity.

Rule: Failed Luhn cards are ignored from redaction & increment the Blocked telemetry counter.
02 / Aadhaar 12-Digit UIDAI

Indian Aadhaar

Matches 12 digits beginning with 2–9 in 4-4-4 groupings with spaces, hyphens, or unbroken numbers.

Boundaries: Lookarounds prevent ingestion inside 16-digit discarded card numbers or order hashes.
03 / PAN Income Tax ID

Indian PAN

Strict structural match on \b[A-Z]{5}\d{4}[A-Z]\b identifying personal and corporate Indian tax accounts.

Case: Normalized to uppercase during pseudonym mapping for deterministic grouping.
04 / Email RFC 5322 TLD

Email Addresses

Matches standard corporate and public mailboxes with validated subdomains and 2+ character top-level domain boundaries.

Case insensitivity: Lowercased keys ensure user@x.com and USER@X.COM share placeholders.
05 / IP v4 Bounded & v6

IP Addresses

Bounded IPv4 (octets rigorously restricted to 0–255 to reject invalid addresses like 999.12.1.1) plus 8-group and compressed (::) IPv6.

Masking: Retains first octet for IPv4 (e.g., 192.XXX.XXX.XXX) and leading segment for IPv6.
06 / Phone E.164 & National

Phone Numbers

Indian mobile formats (+91, 0091, 0, or bare 10-digits starting 6–9 in 5+5 blocks) and North American / international standards with lookarounds.

Normalization: Last-10 digit hashing unifies prefixed variants to the same pseudonym token.
